THE OPPORTUNITY

Premium visual real estate is already there.

Shopping centres, airports, mixed-use developments and transport hubs move thousands of people across escalators every day. Riders are physically carried alongside the glass balustrade for a predictable period of time, yet that surface is commercially dormant.

Elevate turns that dormant space into a transparent digital media surface without changing the essential function of the escalator.

The objective is not to sell screens. The objective is to build a media network.

THE CURRENT WALL
R7.5m

Capital requirement for the South African commercial proof-of-concept phase.

This is not a customer problem.

The immediate bottleneck is funding the physical commercial proof of concept that proves the model in the real world.

The POC should establish real installation cost, reliability, audience behaviour, advertising demand, occupancy, operating cost, margins and payback.

Once that exists, the conversation changes from financing an idea to financing replication of an operating media asset.

I do not want to solve a R7.5m problem today by creating a R100m ownership problem later.

WHY THE US MATTERS

The South African model cannot simply be converted into dollars.

US economics need to be built from zero: installation, escalator compliance, electrical certification, insurance, liability, labour, import costs, property requirements and local media pricing.
